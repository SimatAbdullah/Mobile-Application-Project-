# Loopit – Style Guide

## Colors
- Primary: Blue-violet (#504DCC) – Primary buttons, links, selected navigation items, and recycling icon.
- Accent Blue: #2382BA – Collection-point icons and challenge progress.
- Gold: #DF911A – Rewards, stars, and points highlights.
- Success: #504DCC with a checkmark.
- Error: #D73431.
- Pending: Neutral gray.
- Status is also communicated through icons and text.

## Typography
- Font: Plus Jakarta Sans.
- Screen titles: 18px bold.
- Section headings: 16–20px bold.
- Body text: 16px.
- Labels: 12–14px.
- Points total: 36px extra-bold.

## Spacing and Shapes
- Spacing scale: 4/8px.
- Horizontal screen margins: 20px.
- Card padding: 16px.
- Card corners: 16px.
- Buttons: Pill-shaped.

## Planned Jetpack Compose Components
- TopAppBar
- Button
- OutlinedButton
- Card
- Checkbox
- NavigationBar
- LinearProgressIndicator

These design choices support consistency across Loopit's screens, navigation, recycling history, rewards, and checklist interactions.
